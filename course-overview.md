# AI Power Users — Course Overview

A one-week, hands-on intensive from the **Global Builders Club**. Go from "I've
heard of ChatGPT" to running your own private AI agent, prompting like a pro,
extending models with your own data, automating real work, and protecting your
privacy — with tools that are free and, wherever possible, open source.

**No coding experience required.** Every session is streamed live on YouTube and
supported by a Luma cohort for peer learning.

---

## Format

- **5 days** — Monday to Friday
- **3 hours per day**, delivered as **three 45-minute modules** with a **15-minute break** after each
- **15 modules total**, each one live on YouTube
- **Peer-to-peer learning**: exercises, assignments, and short tests that are peer-evaluated
- **Hands-on every module** — you finish each session with something you built

### Daily shape (3 hours)

| Block | Length | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Module A | 45 min | New concept + guided build (live) |
| Break | 15 min | |
| Module B | 45 min | Hands-on exercises + troubleshooting |
| Break | 15 min | |
| Module C | 45 min | Peer exercises, assignments, and a peer-evaluated check |

---

## The six values

The culture of the room. They shape every lab, critique, and feedback circle.

1. **Win and help win** — Your success and your peers' success are the same goal. You're graded partly on the feedback you *give*.
2. **The Four Agreements** — Be impeccable with your word · Don't take anything personally · Don't make assumptions · Always do your best. The ground rules for every feedback circle.
3. **Criticise in private, praise in public** — Corrections go one-to-one; recognition goes to the whole room.
4. **Create more than you consume** — Ship things; don't just watch demos. Every module ends with something you built.
5. **Done over perfect** — A shipped rough project beats a perfect plan.
6. **Participate** — The class is a practice, not a lecture.

## Assessment

- **Hands-on completion** in every module.
- **Peer-evaluated checks** at the end of each peer lab: a short quiz plus
  open-ended questions answered in your own words and scored by a peer against a
  shared rubric.
- **Capstone** (Module 15): a working demo + one-page write-up.

The peer-evaluation rubric and quizzes live inside each module folder.
