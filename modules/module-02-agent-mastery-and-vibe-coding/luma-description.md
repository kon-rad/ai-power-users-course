## Full description

**This is Module 2 of AI Power Users — a free, hands-on live course from Argo.**

In Module 1 you built a private AI second brain and wrote your first agent skill by hand.
**You never have to do that again.**

This session has two halves, and both run on the same idea: **you describe, the agent
builds.**

**First — your agent builds its own tools.** We give it one brief and it writes three
skills for itself:

- **`/models-research`** — researches the entire AI model landscape once a week. Text, image, video,
  voice, speech-to-text, music. Live pricing, ranked, written into your notes. No more
  "best AI tools" listicles.
- **`/model-switch`** — moves between four models in one command: private, cheap, frontier, and
  the best coding model. It'll stop you before you send client data to a public provider.
- **`/spend-report`** — tracks exactly what your AI usage costs. It remembers what it already
  counted, so every run picks up precisely where the last one stopped.

All three plug into the standup you built in Module 1 — **weekly, not daily**, because the
model landscape moves in weeks and a daily research pass just burns tokens telling you the
same thing five times. When nothing's due, your standup says nothing about them at all.

You'll also learn the single habit that makes an AI agent cheaper *and* smarter: **one task
per session.** We'll explain exactly what that means, why resetting between jobs is free
money, and why you should never do it in the middle of one.

**Then — your agent builds a client's website.** We take a real local business, a fence
installation company, from a blank folder to a **live, deployed website** in one sitting:

1. **Set the goals** — what does this site have to achieve, and how would we know it failed?
2. **Build the knowledge base** — interview the client, and let the agent *look at* their
   photos and write the alt text and a shot list
3. **Design the layout and functionality** — you describe it out loud, the agent writes
   the spec
4. **Pick the architecture** — and make the AI argue against its own recommendation
5. **Approve the plan** — the moment a non-programmer gets their power back
6. **Build it** — with the best coding model, using the real photos
7. **Prove it works** — evidence, not assurances
8. **Git, deploy, live URL** — on camera, on a phone

**An agency quotes this at $4,000–8,000 and six to eight weeks. We'll do it in about an
hour, and hosting is free.**

**By the end you'll have:**
- Three agent-built skills, wired into your standup on a weekly cadence
- A tuned `SOUL.md` — your agent's personality — and you'll know what it costs per message
- A complete client knowledge base: goals, brief, image inventory, layout, architecture
- **A real website, on a real URL, that you can show someone**
- Git set up, so nothing you build is ever lost

**Who it's for:** anyone who finished Module 1, or anyone with a working AI agent and a
willingness to follow along. **No coding experience required.**

**The honest part:** what we build in an hour is an excellent starting point, not a finished
client delivery. Real client work adds their photos, their copy, their domain, and
revisions. But the hour we save is the hour of *building* — which was never the valuable
hour. **The valuable hour is working out what to build, and that's the part we'll spend the
most time on.**

**Format:** One live session, about 110 minutes, with a break in the middle. Live on
YouTube, with peer learning, homework, and a peer-evaluated check.
