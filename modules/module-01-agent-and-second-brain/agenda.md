## Homework (briefed at 0:43, due Module 3)

1. Finish the setup if any piece didn't connect (troubleshooting help comes in Module 2).
2. Run your **daily standup** skill at least twice before the peer lab.
3. Capture 5 real notes into your PARA folders (at least one Project).
4. Complete the Module 1 quiz — bring your open-ended answers for peer review.

## Contingency (if downloads run long)

- Prioritise **Morpheus account + API key** and **Hermes ↔ Morpheus connection** live; these have no big download.
- Obsidian vault + PARA can be done while Handy/models finish downloading.
- The **daily-standup skill** is the payoff — protect the last 7 minutes for it.
