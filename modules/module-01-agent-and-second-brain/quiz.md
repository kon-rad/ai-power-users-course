# Module 1 — Knowledge Check

Two parts:

- **Part A** — 5 multiple-choice questions (quick self-check, answer key at the bottom).
- **Part B** — 5 open-ended questions you answer **in your own words**, then
  swap with a peer for evaluation using the rubric.

This is peer-to-peer learning: explaining a concept in your own words is the best
test that you actually understand it.

---

## Part A — Multiple choice

**1. What is the main purpose of the PARA method in your second brain?**
- A) To encrypt your notes
- B) To organise everything into four buckets: Projects, Areas, Resources, Archives
- C) To back up your notes to the cloud
- D) To convert speech to text

**2. Why do we launch the Hermes agent from *inside* your `SecondBrain` folder?**
- A) So it runs faster
- B) So its working directory is your notes, letting it read and write them
- C) Because Hermes can only be installed there
- D) So Obsidian can see the agent

**3. What role does Morpheus play in the stack?**
- A) It's the note-taking app
- B) It's the speech-to-text engine
- C) It's the private, decentralized model (the "brain") that powers the agent
- D) It's the terminal you run agents in

**4. Handy keeps your voice private because it…**
- A) Encrypts audio before uploading it to Handy's servers
- B) Runs speech-to-text completely offline on your own machine
- C) Only works when you're disconnected from the internet
- D) Deletes recordings after 24 hours

**5. You connect Hermes to Morpheus by setting which two things?**
- A) A username and password
- B) `OPENAI_BASE_URL` (`https://api.mor.org/api/v1`) and `OPENAI_API_KEY` (your Morpheus key)
- C) Your Wi-Fi name and a phone number
- D) The Obsidian vault path and a theme

## Part B — Explain in your own words (peer-evaluated)

Write a few sentences for each. Don't quote the guide — use your own words.

**1.** In plain language, what is a "private AI second brain," and how is it
different from just using a chatbot on a website?

**2.** Describe what each of the six tools does and how they connect: **Handy,
Obsidian, VS Code, Hermes, Morpheus, cmux.**

**3.** Explain the PARA method. Give one real example of something in your own
life that would go in each of the four buckets.

**4.** What is a Hermes **skill**? Describe, in your own words, what your
`daily-standup` skill does step by step.

**5.** Why might someone choose a private/decentralized model like Morpheus over a
mainstream cloud chatbot? Give at least one benefit and one trade-off.

---

## Peer-evaluation rubric (Part B)

For each open-ended answer, the reviewer gives a score of **1–5** and writes **one
strength** and **one suggestion**. Remember the value: *criticise in private,
praise in public.*

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 5 | Clear, correct, and in their own words; shows real understanding with a good example. |
| 4 | Correct and clear, minor gaps or a missing example. |
| 3 | Mostly correct but vague, or leans on memorised phrases. |
| 2 | Partial understanding; a key idea is missing or wrong. |
| 1 | Not yet — mostly incorrect or left blank. |

**Total:** 25 points across the 5 questions. A peer passes the check at **15+**,
with encouragement to revisit anything scored 2 or below before moving on.

**Reviewer checklist:**
- Is it in their **own words** (not copied)?
- Are the **six tools and their roles** correct in Q2?
- Does the **PARA example** in Q3 make sense?
- Does Q4 correctly describe **reading Projects → asking questions → saving a dated note**?
- Does Q5 name a real **benefit *and* a trade-off**?
