## Full description

**This is Module 1 of AI Power Users — a free, hands-on, 5-day live course from
the Global Builders Club.**

Most people use AI by typing into someone else's website. This week you'll do
something different: build your own **private AI second brain** — a personal AI
agent that lives inside *your* notes and thinks with a *private, decentralized*
model. Your notes and your voice stay on your machine, with no big-tech cloud in
the middle. (Running the model itself fully offline is a Day 5 topic.)

In this first 45-minute session we set the whole thing up together, live, step by
step. You'll leave with a working private-AI workstation and your first custom
agent skill.

**What we'll build (the six pieces):**
- 🎙️ **Handy** — talk instead of type, 100% offline speech-to-text
- 🧠 **Obsidian** — your notes as a visual, linked second brain (PARA method)
- 🧩 **VS Code** — inspect the machinery: your skills, config, and files
- 🤖 **Hermes** — an open-source AI agent that lives in your folder
- 🔐 **Morpheus** — a private, decentralized model powering it all
- 💻 **cmux** — a terminal built for running AI agents

**By the end you'll have:**
- The full stack installed and wired together
- A PARA-organised knowledge base in Obsidian
- Your agent connected to a private model
- **Your first skill:** a daily standup where your AI reviews your projects, goals,
  and progress with you — and saves the notes for you

**Who it's for:** anyone curious about AI. **No coding experience required.**
Everything we use is free, and almost all of it is open source.

**Format:** 5 days (Mon–Fri), 3 hours a day, three 45-minute modules per day with
breaks. Live on YouTube, with peer learning, exercises, and peer-evaluated checks.
This event is **Day 1, Module 1 of 15**.
