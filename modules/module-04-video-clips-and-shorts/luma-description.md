## Full description

This is Module 4 of AI Power Users, a free hands on live course from Argo. This one runs
on macOS.

Module 3 gave your agent a camera. This one gives it an editing bench for real footage.

Two halves.

**First, your agent gets a live editing bench.** From one brief, on your own machine, it
writes `/job-reel`, a skill that turns a raw job video into a captioned vertical short in
the project you are already looking at.

**Then it edits real footage.**

1. Confirm ArgoCut and the browser tab are live.
2. Build `/job-reel` from one brief.
3. Add a caption and title preset from your client's colours.
4. Feed it a real job video, probe and transcribe it.
5. Approve a cut plan built from sentence boundaries, not seconds.
6. Watch the agent assemble the clip live, in your own tab.

This is the first module that costs 0 USD. ArgoCut, the transcription, and the browser tab
are all local.

**By the end you will have:**

- A skill, `/job-reel`, that turns any job video into a captioned vertical short
- A `fence-co` caption and title preset
- **One captioned vertical short, assembled live, in a project you own**
- An `edit-plan.md` for every clip
- A working local ArgoCut install

**Who it is for:** anyone who finished Module 3, or anyone with a working agent and real
footage to edit.

**The honest part:** there is no export button. The skill assembles the clip and stops.
Rendering a client facing video stays a decision you make by hand, in the ArgoCut UI, not
the agent's.

**Format:** one live session, 60 minutes, no break. Live on YouTube. macOS.
